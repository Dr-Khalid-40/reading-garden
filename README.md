### 📚 Reading Garden

**Reading Garden is a colorful, personal reading companion designed to turn reading into a habit worth growing. 🌱📖**

Track your books, reading progress, pages, reading time, goals, notes, highlights, and daily reading activity — all in one beautiful dashboard. The app gives you a clear picture of your reading journey while keeping the experience simple, motivating, and enjoyable.

✨ **Features**

* 📚 Personal book library
* 📖 Track current page and reading progress
* ⏱️ Log reading sessions, pages, and time
* 🎯 Set and track reading goals
* 📅 Interactive reading calendar
* 📊 Reading statistics and progress insights
* 📝 Notes & highlights
* 🌱 Reading streaks and activity tracking
* 🌈 Colorful light mode + dark mode
* 🔐 User authentication
* ☁️ Supabase-powered cloud storage
* 🔄 Sync your reading data across devices
* 📱 Responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile
